# Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of marketing campaign performance, focusing on campaign efficiency, audience behaviour, creative performance, and channel-level outcomes.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of campaign performance through:

- Revenue
- Ad Spend
- Profit
- ROAS
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue vs Ad Spend by Platform
- ROAS by Platform
- Monthly Revenue & Profit Trends
- Profit by Campaign Objective

### 2. Audience Performance Analysis

Examines campaign performance across audience segments:

- ROAS by Age Group
- Conversion Rate by Purchase Intent
- Profit by Interest Category
- Retargeting Performance

Interactive filters allow analysis by gender, income, purchase intent, and retargeting status.

### 3. Creative & Campaign Analysis

Evaluates the relationship between creative characteristics and campaign performance:

- ROAS by Creative Format
- CTR by Ad Copy Length
- Conversion Rate by Creative Emotion
- Conversion Rate by Call-to-Action
